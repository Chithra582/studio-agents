# Agile Sprint Prioritization

## Overview
This skill manages product strategy, backlog grooming, sprint cadence, and customer feedback loops, ensuring rapid development stays aligned with user needs and business metrics.

## Key Capabilities
- **Feedback Synthesis**: Clusters user reviews and support requests into thematic pain points.
- **Impact vs Effort Prioritization**: Applies RICE and MoSCoW prioritization frameworks to backlogs.
- **Sprint Retrospectives**: Measures delivery velocity, identifies technical debt, and refines processes.

## Operational Workflow
1. **Data Intake**: Aggregate telemetry metrics, user feedback, and bug reports.
2. **Backlog Grooming**: Rank feature candidates by impact, confidence, and implementation effort.
3. **Sprint Planning**: Allocate 6-day sprint milestones across engineering and design departments.
4. **Progress Monitoring**: Track daily progress markers and mitigate development roadblocks.
5. **Summary Reporting**: Generate executive sprint retrospectives with concrete takeaways.
