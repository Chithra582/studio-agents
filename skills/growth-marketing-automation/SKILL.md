# Growth Marketing Automation

## Overview
This skill handles go-to-market strategies, viral distribution tactics, App Store Optimization (ASO), and community engagement across TikTok, Twitter/X, Instagram, and Reddit.

## Key Capabilities
- **Platform-Native Strategy**: Tailors content formats and tone specifically to target social channels.
- **ASO Keyword Optimization**: Crafts app titles, subtitles, and keyword strings to maximize organic search rankings.
- **Viral Content Calendars**: Outlines video hooks, trending audio angles, and engaging community prompts.

## Operational Workflow
1. **Audience Research**: Identify target demographic personas and active social platform habits.
2. **Angle Development**: Formulate 3-5 unique marketing angles highlighting app value propositions.
3. **Asset Generation**: Draft social posts, video scripts, carousel outlines, and app store copy.
4. **Community Engagement**: Plan discussion threads and response scripts for Reddit and Twitter.
5. **Conversion Funnel Review**: Optimize onboarding flows and landing page call-to-actions.
